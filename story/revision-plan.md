# Second edit: editorial rebuild

## Diagnosis

The old 2-second contact sheets show repeated cream frames, a permanent masthead/progress/footer, a phone at the same scale, and successive cards. In several runs of six thumbnails the only visible change is a caption. The opening also spends too long on one orbit image. This is a structural editing problem, so the shared `Base` scene layout will be retired for this cut.

## Eight new sequences

1. **Hook / origin:** real X Doctor artwork and the actual episode, then a quick search-to-player transformation; give glimpses of Notes, comments, and listening history before the title.
2. **Player:** use the supplied player capture as an active surface. Move from cover to play control to timeline and back to the whole screen. Claims stay limited to visible UI.
3. **Discovery:** use official feed/search images and an expanding group of real episode artwork. Daqing, if mentioned, is one passing example.
4. **Intimacy:** cut to licensed real commute photography; make the audio wave and visual space respond to the voice rather than relying on an AI illustration.
5. **Show Notes:** begin with the actual text screen, push through the photo screen, then let the 2008 photo and DVD-shop photo emerge from their source. End by returning to the episode.
6. **Comments:** magnify the real 02:33 timestamp, move into the redacted comment screen, draw short, faithful listener-response fragments from visible content, then return to the playback moment.
7. **Listening trail:** reveal 128 h 27 m as total listening time; separately zoom into the 100-hour creator-specific sticker and profile placement. The two metrics must never be equated.
8. **Judgement / ending:** demonstrate the phone-first preference and Chinese-language discovery context as a personal judgement. Finish with a rapid visual callback through the materials visited, ending on the one-to-many thesis.

Every sequence will contain internal shot changes, crop pushes, match cuts, or composition changes. Visual events target 1–3 seconds, major layout changes 4–7 seconds; motion must move the argument forward.
